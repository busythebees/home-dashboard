ALS Privacy Policy

ALS is a private personal application used by its owner to manage tasks, events and other personal information.

Google account data

ALS uses read-only access to a dedicated Gmail account to read emails received by that account.

Email data is used to identify information relevant to the owner's personal task, event and scheduling system.

Use of data

Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the personal functionality of ALS. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Data sharing

ALS does not provide Google account data to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Access

Google account access can be revoked at any time through the Google account's security settings.

This application is intended solely for personal use by its owner.
